Protocol

A community seal for claims.

The problem

Claims move faster than proof.

  • A reader, a forum, and a marketplace each invent their own verified mark.
  • Those marks do not travel.
  • Being wrong costs nothing.

The protocol

Lock tokens on a claim. Earn the seal.

The community validates a specific statement. Reputation weights the lock. Cross the threshold, and any app can read the same seal.

How a seal is issued

Four moves.

01

Open

A piece is a claim, not the whole article. News or project.

02

Lock

Curators lock CUR for or against it. Tokens sit in the piece vault.

03

Weight

The lock is multiplied by the curator’s reputation, fixed at that moment.

04

Seal

Net weight of 560 or more issues the seal. The other side can reject it.

The weight

Reputation changes the vote.

weight = amount × (0.60 + reputation / 1000)

Distribution

One state. Three surfaces.

Reader

The newsroom does not grant the seal. It reads it under the headline.

Forum

The thread shows the same status beside the claim.

Marketplace

A project listing carries the seal, or shows that curation is still open.
